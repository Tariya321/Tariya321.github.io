During the past 2 centuries, there are 3 main technologies that greatly changed our life, they are: steam engine, electricity and computer. 

However, it has been more than 70 years since the last leap, we want to ask: what is the next? what is the next technology that will eventually changed our life? The answer is today's presentation topic: the generative AI, the leading force of the 4th industrial revolution.

At the past decade, we see the rapid development of generative AI. In Jan. of 2023, OpenAi lanuched their product: ChatGPT. A big bang of AI. 
Since then, lots of great companies like Google and Meta are speed up to roll into this revolution. and now, 9 in 10 students will use generative AI to help themself.

Basicly, one can think generative AI is a black box, and what you have to do is to give a question (or instruction), like: Hi, Siri. WHAT is the weather of tomorrow? And the output will the right one.

The good news is: GAI can do things far more than this! and we have seen lots of progress.!
